What is sold

Written diagnosis, $250, paid in advance. A written analysis of one broken payment or automation workflow (for example a Stripe webhook, an n8n, Make or Zapier flow, a queue, or a retry path) that contains: a reproduction of the failure, the mechanism that causes it, and the exact fix. What arrives is listed on the delivery checklist. It is delivered by email as a written document and, where useful, as code in a repository you control.

Technical writing. Articles are commissioned per piece under a separate written agreement with the publication.

The 48-hour clock

The diagnosis is delivered within 48 hours of receiving the materials it needs: the failing workflow or repository, read access or an export, the last three failures with timestamps, and one person to ask questions. The clock starts when those arrive, not when payment arrives. Weekends count. If more material is needed, you will be asked once, in writing, and the clock pauses until it arrives.

Refunds

  • No reproducible cause found: if the delivered diagnosis does not identify a reproducible cause for the failure you reported, you can ask for a full refund within 7 days of delivery and receive it.
  • After delivery, otherwise: the diagnosis is a work product delivered in full, so it is not refundable once delivered, except as above.
  • Before work starts: if you cancel before sending materials, full refund.
  • Fix work: refunds follow the milestones in its written quote.

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe, usually within 5 to 10 business days. To request one, email shabbal1@charlotte.edu with the order reference.
